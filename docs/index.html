Judgment over jargon

The four habits
behind great PMs.

Use these as a review lens on every brief, roadmap, experiment, and launch.

01

Start with evidence

Separate what you know, what you infer, and what must still be tested.

02

Make the choice explicit

A strategy is meaningful only when it rules out attractive alternatives.

03

Instrument the downside

Pair every success metric with a guardrail that exposes who or what might lose.

04

Close the learning loop

A shipped feature is unfinished until the team knows what changed and why.
